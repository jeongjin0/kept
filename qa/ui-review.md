# UI review · 0.6.0

Captured 2026-10-13 from the built extension. Popup height 426 px in both languages. Settings at 1440 px and 420 px. The skip chip on a live signed-out YouTube Short.

## What changed

The accent moved from #FF4D2E, about twice as saturated as everything around it, to a teal at the same lightness and chroma as every other candidate hue. The dark surfaces, text and borders lean the same way by a very small amount. The mark is now three streaks of unequal strength behind a play triangle: videos passed, then the next one. The toolbar icon, the popup and settings header, the chip's dot and the store images use it.

## What holds

The popup is still one screen with nothing to scroll. Settings is one column and its rows keep their layout at 420 px. The chip stays centred on the video and readable over a bright title card.

## Still open

Other hues, and how the accent reads on displays other than the one used for these captures. A signed-in check of subscribe and follow.
